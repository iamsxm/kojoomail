Kojoo Mail

一个窗口，收所有邮箱

Kojoo Mail 是 Windows 和 macOS 上的桌面邮件客户端。Gmail、Outlook、QQ、网易、iCloud 和企业邮箱都能加进来，新邮件按时间排在一起；可选的 AI 帮你写摘要、提取待办、起草回复。

Kojoo Mail is a desktop email client for Windows and macOS. Add Gmail, Outlook, QQ, NetEase, iCloud and work mailboxes, read them in one unified inbox, and optionally let AI summarise messages, extract to-dos and draft replies.

数据在你电脑上

邮件只缓存在本机，密码和登录授权存在系统的凭据管理器（Windows 凭据管理器 / macOS 钥匙串）。我们不收集任何数据；软件联网只用来收发邮件、调用你自己配置的 AI 和检查更新。

Mail is cached only on your computer; credentials live in the system credential store (Windows Credential Manager / macOS Keychain). We collect no data; the app goes online only to send and receive your mail, call the AI service you configure, and check for updates.

一键登录 Gmail

用谷歌官方登录页授权，密码只在谷歌页面输入。授权只用来收发你自己的邮件。

Sign in with Google's own consent page. Access is used only to send and receive your own mail.

AI 可选，用你自己的密钥

AI 只在你配置了自己的模型密钥后才工作，每个邮箱都能单独关掉。应用内 AI 只起草，从不替你发信。

AI works only with your own model API key and can be turned off per account. In-app AI only drafts — it never sends mail on your behalf.
